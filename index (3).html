About SudoZen

SudoZen is a Sudoku game made for a calm, focused experience. It has four difficulty levels, a daily puzzle, a performance dashboard, notes, hints, undo, and saved statistics.

  1. Goal: fill the grid so each row, column, and 3×3 box has the numbers 1–9 once.
  2. Select: tap an empty cell. With highlighting on, its row, column, box, and matching numbers light up.
  3. Enter: tap a number under the board or press 1–9. Erase or Backspace removes it.
  4. Mistakes: wrong numbers turn red and add to your mistake count. If you reach the limit, the game ends.
  5. Notes (N): write small possible numbers in a cell.
  6. Undo (U): takes back your last move.
  7. Hints: 3 per game. A hint fills a cell correctly and lowers your score.
  8. Dashboard: time, score, accuracy, progress, and a tracker for how many of each number remain.
  9. Daily Challenge: one shared puzzle per day. Your game is saved so you can Continue later.
  10. Win: complete every cell correctly to see your score.

How to Play

  1. Goal: fill the grid so each row, column, and 3×3 box has the numbers 1–9 once.
  2. Select: click or tap an empty cell. Its row, column, and box light up, and matching numbers glow.
  3. Enter: tap a number under the board or press 1–9. Use Erase or Backspace to remove it.
  4. Wrong answers turn red and shake. They are added to your mistake count on the dashboard.
  5. Notes: turn on Notes (or press N) to write small possible numbers in a cell.
  6. Hints: you have 3. A hint fills the selected cell with the correct number and lowers your score.
  7. Dashboard: shows time, mistakes, hints left, progress, and how many of each number you still need.
  8. Pause stops the timer and hides the board.
  9. Win: complete every cell to see your time and score.
